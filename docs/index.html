From order to reconciliation, nine steps, fully orchestrated

  1. 1
    Order Ingestion
    Orders flow in from your storefront, marketplace, or ERP via API or connector.
  2. 2
    Smart Carrier Allocation
    The allocation engine scores every eligible carrier and assigns the best match.
  3. 3
    Label & Manifest Generation
    Shipping labels and carrier manifests are generated automatically.
  4. 4
    Pickup Scheduling
    Pickup requests are scheduled with the assigned carrier for your warehouse.
  5. 5
    In-Transit Tracking
    Every shipment status update is normalized into one tracking timeline.

    NDR & Exception Management

  7. 7
    Delivery Confirmation
    Proof of delivery is captured and synced back to your system.
  8. 8

  9. 9

    Cost, performance, and COD reconciliation data roll up into reporting.
